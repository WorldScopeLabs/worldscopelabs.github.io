# Privacy Policy for WorldScope Labs Apps

**Last updated:** August 2026

WorldScope Labs ("we", "our", or "us") develops mobile applications and games. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our services.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Our applications are designed with privacy in mind:
- We do **not** collect personally identifiable information (such as name, address, or government ID).
- We may use third-party services (such as Google Play Services, Unity Ads, or AdMob) that collect information used to identify your device for analytics and advertising purposes.

### 2. Log Data & Analytics
In case of an error or crash in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This may include device model, OS version, and crash timestamps to help us improve stability.

### 3. Third-Party Services
Our apps may contain links or SDKs to third-party services:
- [Google Play Services](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=tr)

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
